About Us

Global Mission Everywhere, Every Time, Everyone is an emerging Christian mission initiative based in Ethiopia, with a vision to serve beyond borders. We want to work alongside churches and believers to share the Good News of Jesus Christ, support evangelism and discipleship, and equip people for mission.

Our Vision

A world where people everywhere have the opportunity to hear the Gospel and grow as disciples of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To connect people, churches, and mission partners for prayer, evangelism, discipleship, and compassionate service.

Our Values

Faithfulness to Scripture, prayer, humility, partnership, responsible stewardship, and respect for every person.
